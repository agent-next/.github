# Direction

A statement of direction, not a release schedule. It changes as we learn.

## Where we are going

Agent-native systems in which large teams of agents carry out long-horizon work autonomously in the cloud. People set direction, oversee execution, and provide feedback; agents do the work.

## Questions we are working on

- **Operation at scale.** What infrastructure lets many agents work in parallel for hours or days, and recover on their own when something fails?
- **Verification.** How can agent work be checked well enough that people can oversee outcomes instead of individual steps?
- **Oversight.** What should people see, and when should they intervene, as they oversee a large team of autonomous agents?
- **Learning from experience.** How can agents improve from outcomes, feedback, and failures across runs?
- **Applications.** Where do autonomous agents do useful work first, in science and beyond, and what counts as a checkable result there?
